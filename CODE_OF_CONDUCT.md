# Shiden Code of Conduct

This code of conduct applies to all Shiden community spaces, including issue trackers, discussions, pull requests, and anything related to the project.

## TL;DR

- Be respectful and inclusive.
- Focus on constructive feedback.
- No harassment or discrimination.
- Report issues to maintainers.
- Be civil and humane.

## Our Goal

Shiden is about building a compiler and language that anyone can learn from, experiment with, and contribute to. We want a community where everyone feels respected and welcome.

## Be Respectful

* Critique ideas, do not attack people.
* Avoid insults, harassment, or dismissive behavior.
* Help others learn instead of breaking them down.

## Be Inclusive

* Everyone is welcome, no matter their background.
* No discrimination based on race, gender, nationality, or identity.
* Encourage and support newcomers.

## Be Constructive

* Provide feedback with suggestions for improvement.
* Stay focused on solutions.
* Keep discussions on topic and relevant to Shiden.

## Unacceptable Behavior

* Harassment or personal attacks.
* Discrimination or exclusion.
* Trolling or deliberat disruptive behavior.
* Spam or irrelevant advertising.
* Deliberate misinformation.

## Reporting Issues

If you see or experience unacceptable behavior:

* Report it to us (the project maintainers).
* Provide context or examples if possible.
* Reports will be handled respectfully and confidentially.
* There is no retaliation for reporting in good faith.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:

1. A warning.
2. Temporary exclusion.
3. Removal from community spaces for serious or repeated issues.

The goal is to maintain a safe, welcoming, and productive environment.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all Shiden-related spaces, online or offline.

## Acknowledgment

By participating in the Shiden community, you agree to follow this code of conduct.
